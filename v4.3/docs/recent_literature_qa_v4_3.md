# Recent Literature QA for Revision v4.3

The comparison table contains ten peer-reviewed studies from 2022-2026. Bibliographic metadata were checked against Crossref and publisher or PubMed records. The two papers explicitly requested by Reviewer 1 are included as `LIT01` and `LIT02`.

The revised manuscript must not compare reported determination coefficients as though all studies solve the same task. The recent literature spans at least four distinct settings:

1. Controlled manufacturing-process prediction, where surface topography and chemistry are measured directly.
2. Domain-specific geological wettability prediction using pressure, temperature, salinity, mineralogy, or well logs.
3. Image-based extraction of a contact angle from a droplet contour.
4. Physics-based estimation for specified smooth-solid/liquid systems.

The present task is different: it predicts a target liquid after removing that liquid from the same-surface probe set and assesses transfer across publication sources. The Introduction should describe the accomplishments of the cited studies, while the Discussion should explain this task mismatch before interpreting their lower within-domain errors or higher R2 values.
